# Homelab: governing philosophy

## Purpose

Homelab is a personal capability system: an adaptable collection of software, tools, intelligence and compute resources whose purpose is to increase the owner's intellectual independence, practical accomplishment and well-being. The platform must evolve to serve changing goals rather than demand that the owner conform to its internal organization.

Two outcomes govern every major product decision:

1. **Greater capability.** Strengthen durable understanding, independent reasoning, problem solving, skill acquisition, creativity and the ability to produce excellent work.
2. **Greater agency over attention.** Remove unnecessary clerical work, context switching, duplication and administrative friction so scarce attention can be devoted to difficult and meaningful activity.

Efficiency is not the objective by itself. Time saved is valuable insofar as it improves thoughtful work, rest, relationships, health or freely chosen pursuits.

## Design obligations

- **A single intelligible product.** The owner interacts with one coherent system. Internal services may have independent security, failure and database boundaries, but do not require separate assistants or contradictory resource identities.
- **Adaptability.** Behavior, goals, connected tools, schedules, hardware, permitted actions and preferences are configurable, versioned and reversible. New requirements should not demand rebuilding the architecture.
- **Evidence before assurance.** An explanation is not mastery; a queued task is not completion; a planned workout is not a performed workout; a suggested meal is not a consumed meal; an AI opinion is not verified research.
- **Reasoning from foundations.** Teach why principles follow from definitions, assumptions and observations. Use difficult varied transfer problems, explanation, counterexamples and delayed assessment to develop general problem-solving ability rather than recognition of familiar forms.
- **Respect for personal autonomy.** Offer consented recommendations and proportionate prompts; the owner sets priorities, permissible nudging, quiet hours and opt-out controls. Do not manipulate emotion, invent motives or turn self-improvement into constant notifications.
- **Offload the mechanical, retain the consequential.** Automate logging, classification, scheduling suggestions, data organization and repetitive calculations where the evidence is reliable. Keep personal judgment, scientific conclusions, financial/clinical decisions and destructive infrastructure control under appropriate human authority.
- **Safety at the owner boundary.** The assistant never inherits unrestricted infrastructure, device, external-account or private-data credentials. Sensitive operations are independently authorized, scoped, auditable and revocable.
- **Sustainable progress.** Protect sleep, health, relationships and restorative time. Optimize for long-term effectiveness rather than an ever-expanding task queue.

## Personal development

### Research and engineering

Make ordinary ChatGPT capable of repository exploration, professional code editing, project planning, issue and PR management, bounded compute execution and repeatable development workflows through Homelab. Projects may link multiple repositories and reusable research libraries. The **Development** skill is the canonical method for maintainable work, while the independent security and release boundaries preserve explicit authority.

Knowledge should connect claims, evidence, experiments, assumptions, hypotheses, mistakes and open questions. Every conclusion should remain traceable to its source and confidence; research memory must support revision rather than calcify mistakes.

### Learning and creative practice

Build a persistent record of demonstrated knowledge, prerequisite relationships, learner-authored proofs and attempts, unresolved misconceptions, transfer performance and delayed retention. Select the shortest defensible path from established foundations to a chosen goal, not the shortest sequence of memorized formulas. Support ongoing piano practice, repertoire selection, musical theory, playing challenges and deliberate review without fabricating performance evidence.

### Time and motivation

Plan around calendar commitments, dependencies, energy, urgency and the actual time available. Distinguish consequential tasks from quick wins without burying the primary task list in habits. Summaries use observed records. The owner can authorize gentle reminders, focus plans and motivational framing, and may disable these without losing task history.

### Health and domestic routines

Reduce the effort of nutrition logging, workout tracking, meal composition, ingredient inventory, shopping, cooking and health trend analysis. Reuse measured data, recipes and explicitly confirmed records. Estimate uncertain nutrients transparently; never treat absent measurements as zero or infer a meal was consumed. Recommend meals using available ingredients, macro/micro targets, time, equipment, taste and preferences; preserve meaningful owner choices.

## How to judge a feature

Before adding a capability, ask:

1. Which owner goal or recurring burden does it address?
2. Does it enhance independent competence or free meaningful attention?
3. What evidence determines whether the recommendation or action succeeded?
4. Can the owner understand, override, revise, revoke and undo its behavior?
5. Does it fit the existing project/resource model and authoritative data owners?
6. Is the recurring maintenance, security and cognitive cost smaller than the benefit?

Prioritize useful capability over feature counts. A feature without an owner outcome, measurement or control mechanism does not satisfy this philosophy.
