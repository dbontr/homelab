# Learning contract

Adaptive Teaching MCP separates three records: domain knowledge, the learner's demonstrated understanding, and the learner's independent problem-solving ability. These records interact but do not substitute for one another. Encountering a statement, receiving an explanation, or uploading completed work never awards mastery by itself.

## Foundational reconstruction

Full mastery requires reconstruction from foundations the learner has already demonstrated. The learner must justify each essential step, handle assumptions, solve unfamiliar problems independently, and demonstrate retention after a meaningful delay. For the software's minimum gate, an unassisted successful retention check must occur at least 24 hours after the latest qualifying independent reconstruction; an intervening reconstruction resets that interval. Passing this minimum is not proof of durable learning across weeks or months.

Start from verified prerequisites instead of an assumed school level. Use strong earlier assessments as evidence of secure foundations. Investigate uncertain prerequisites without repeatedly testing knowledge already demonstrated. Stop the reconstruction at established knowledge, explicit definitions, empirical premises, or declared axioms. Do not impose an endless regress.

For example, algebra permits manipulation of a difference quotient, but algebra alone does not entail differentiation. Defining a derivative also requires a limiting process. The learner should motivate that construction, explain its assumptions, and reconstruct its meaning rather than quote differentiation rules.

Assess essential explanatory inferences independently. Knowing the premise and the conclusion does not demonstrate knowing why the conclusion follows.

## Durable learning sessions

A session binds a declared goal to an existing concept and preserves its learning target, mode, diagnostic responses, plan versions, and reasoning-step assessments. The phases are diagnosis, planning, teaching, practice, reassessment, and completion. A session can conclude without claiming that the learner mastered its goal.

Begin probing at the target rather than at an assumed elementary level. If the response exposes a gap, narrow the investigation toward its uncertain prerequisites. A correct diagnostic response directs instruction but does not establish durable mastery. Build a revisable route from independently demonstrated foundations and recorded justifications. Treat a derivative's limiting process as an explicit premise rather than deriving it from algebra alone.

A reasoning step has an identified conclusion, necessary premises, and an evaluation of the learner's actual argument. A justified link is provisional until its premises and the learner's unassisted inference have adequate evidence. A supported concept and an independently supported reasoning link are distinct. Teaching questions are stored with a committed answer and rubric before presentation. A session preserves pending answers across restarts, refuses a second unresolved question, and will not advance while one remains outstanding. Prediction and comprehension check are separate kinds of work; a graded diagnostic response informs lesson planning without conferring durable mastery. Choose one suitable proof route from valid alternatives instead of requiring every route. Retain earlier evidence when a plan changes. Never convert a teaching-session evaluation into a durable mastery score without independent assessment under the relevant standard.

These mechanics are discipline-neutral. Mathematical deductions, empirical observations, historical arguments, and practical techniques require different forms of evidence. The server records the structure and source. The host judges the work within its competence.

## Teaching and assistance

- **Learn:** Probe near the learner's demonstrated level. Introduce missing foundations, support motivated discovery, and intervene when a meaningful misunderstanding appears.
- **Practise:** Build retrieval, method selection, discrimination, and composition through varied exercises, not conceptual recitation alone.
- **Solve:** Protect extended independent work. Remain silent until the learner requests a hint, review, or final evaluation. Guided mode permits requested intermediate reviews. Exam mode provides no help during the attempt.

An assisted solution can represent progress, but it does not establish unaided competence. Consider the novelty and difficulty of successful work. Two trivial solutions cannot establish mastery of a demanding subject merely because a minimum counter was satisfied. A numeric evidence threshold is a gate, not a complete educational judgment.

## Graph and sources

Propose encountered propositions, source statements, explanations, and connections for inclusion in the domain graph. Treat unverified extraction as provisional material, not as a measurement of the learner. Check premises and citations before relying on them. Keep observations, models, and conclusions distinct. Evaluate reasoning links separately from the claims they connect.

The system applies across disciplines. Mathematical theorems demand deduction. Scientific claims need empirical support. Historical interpretations require source criticism and competing accounts. Languages and practical crafts call for demonstrated performance. Never impose proof-style assessment on every subject.

## Images and other learner work

Accept text and, where the host supports them, photographs of handwritten work, diagrams, experiments, prototypes, objects, and documents. Preserve the original image and its provenance when the server provides a verified durable ingestion path. A transcription or description is derived evidence, not a replacement for the original image.

A host may let the tutor inspect an image without giving the server a way to store it. In that case, explain the limitation. Do not claim the server has saved the original. If the model cannot inspect the image, do not infer its contents or grade it as observed work.

Protect learner material with the same authorization and retention rules as text submissions. Support text-only, image-only, and combined attempts without assuming the subject is mathematics.

## Assessment quality

Distinguish the demonstrated result, the learner's self-reported confidence and the evaluator's confidence. Learners may submit a final answer, a supporting explanation, or both as appropriate to the task. Retain their explanation and confidence with the exact saved submission; absence is unknown, not zero. Stated confidence never increases a mastery score or substitutes for justified reasoning. A correct diagnostic choice without a separately assessed, supported explanation remains inconclusive evidence of prerequisite understanding. The reported outcome of an objective answer and the evaluation of the learner's inference are distinct; never infer a justification from the selected option. Check hidden assumptions, alternative routes, counterexamples, and the scope of the conclusion. Use executable tests, trustworthy sources, calculations, or formal verification when suitable. Record uncertainty, assistance, task difficulty, and delayed reassessment separately. Use self-confidence paired with precommitted answer keys or authenticated checker reports to describe calibration with Brier scores and reliability bands. Preserve separate populations for multiple-choice accuracy and independently checked results, and disclose sample sizes and exclusions. Calibration is descriptive, not a substitute for reasoning assessment or a mastery gate. Never award learner mastery from automatically ingested content or model-authored reasoning.

## Source-grounded reasoning and practice

The domain graph contains provisional propositions with contextual scope, verbatim source quotations and multi-premise reasoning chains. Each reasoning chain preserves a jointly required premise set; a single arrow must not imply that one premise alone is sufficient. A model proposes the extraction and a separate audit adjudicates identity and evidence. Material from a trustworthy text is not automatically true; a true statement is not automatically understood by its reader. A source dispute is represented explicitly rather than resolved by a guess. Contradictory propositions remain linked with their source provenance; reconciliation can record that apparently incompatible claims apply in distinct contexts.

A chain can receive an explicit review only after its conclusion and every premise have individually reviewed source attributions and no blocking source disputes. Its review rationale is retained. Review standing is recalculated from current claim states, provenance, and dispute resolutions, so a subsequent contradiction can block an earlier review without erasing its history. Claims from explicitly incompatible contexts cannot jointly support one undifferentiated argument. This mechanical eligibility check does not certify truth, semantic entailment, or learner mastery.

Deductive claims can additionally carry an exhaustive propositional check, bound to explicit formulas for **all** premises and the conclusion and a documented meaning for each atomic symbol. This identifies countermodels, jointly inconsistent premises, and individually redundant assumptions. The checker is complete only for the represented fragment of classical propositional logic. A result of `valid_on_formalization` is not a certificate that the natural-language statements were faithfully translated; quantifiers, mathematics beyond propositional logic, physical mechanisms, empirical support, and explanatory adequacy require their own discipline-specific validation. An operator's unverified formalization is retained as an auditable claim, never promoted to independent learner mastery evidence.

A translation judgment must cite a valid source span and the original, accepted quotation for every premise and conclusion. The record must preserve exact proposition identity, alignment decisions, explanatory limitations and assumptions, and the reviewer-supplied reference. The system must reject fabricated, missing or altered anchors and retain divergent judgments without silently reconciling them. Later source or proposition changes must invalidate the current binding of an earlier review. Reviewer identity is not authenticated by a self-reported label. The existence of a citation-backed review does not by itself certify that a mathematical formalization, physical model, historical claim or empirical explanation is sound.

Practice records recall, discrimination, composition and extended solving as distinct operations. It must interleave relevant targets, preserve the individual exercise and its sealed reference solution, diagnose the first obstruction, and schedule fresh-surface retests after misses. Repeatedly explaining an idea is not a substitute for doing the work. Full mastery requires foundational reconstruction, several distinct difficult independent solutions, variety, meaningful delay, and authenticated independent-checker corroboration. Provisional reports do not satisfy this requirement. A signature verifies the provenance and scope of a checker report, but does not independently establish proof correctness or sound experimental design.

Original files and model interpretations are independent data. The storage layer protects the original bytes, validates file signatures and associates the material with the exact learning record. The host must actually inspect an image before reasoning from its visible content; unreadable symbols remain uncertain. Practical or historical subjects use domain-appropriate evidence rather than imposed mathematical proof.

The Go server implements durable state and deterministic checks. Host-mediated extraction, practice generation, interpretation and grading happen only when a connected AI host executes the relevant MCP workflow. Automatic updates must not be confused with hidden autonomous model execution.

## Private data continuity

Learning evidence, source provenance and original work are durable user-owned records. Exporting the SQLite database must preserve all of them together, including unanswered assessments and sealed solutions. A backup is a protected copy of private data; it is not a new assessment or a publishing action. Restore success must be tested independently of taking the snapshot.
