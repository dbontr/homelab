# Naturalness, voice, and nonformulaic prose

## What naturalness means

Naturalness is purposeful, context-sensitive writing rather than imitation of surface "human" tics. Do not add errors, contractions, false memories, jokes, anecdotes, slang, random punctuation, fragments, or arbitrary sentence variation as camouflage. A technical specification and a private note may both be natural while sharing almost no stylistic features.

Before revision, identify the destination and choose a profile: **general**, **workplace**, **academic**, **technical**, **legal**, **marketing**, or **personal**. Genre requirements outrank generic heuristics. Legal repetition, a scientific hedge, a defined term, a methodological heading, or an exact instruction may be necessary.

## Voice fingerprint

Identify stable features of the source author's language: register, sentence architecture, syntactic density, characteristic nouns and verbs, imagery, humor, emotional distance, rhythm, favored transitions, willingness to qualify, and rhetorical intensity. Preserve distinctive passages and deliberate deviations. If asked to match a sample, reproduce abstract qualities without copying distinctive sentences or pretending to be the author in a deceptive context.

## High-value diagnostic patterns

Look for clusters rather than banning phrases mechanically:

- Generic openings announcing that a subject is important; significance claimed before mechanism.
- Empty commentary that labels something insightful, nuanced, complex, or revealing without making an inference.
- Phantom authorities such as "experts agree" with no attributable evidence.
- Rigid "not X, but Y" or "not only X, but also Y" used as a default reasoning engine.
- Tricolons, mirrored paragraphs, and enumerated taxonomies where the material does not warrant them.
- Abstract noun chains hiding agents, actions, objects, and constraints.
- Uniform sentence and paragraph lengths, polished relay structures, and excessive explicit transitions.
- Examples manufactured to illustrate every concept cleanly, with suspiciously convenient numbers or resolutions.
- Generic professional or academic language that obscures concrete decisions, methods, and consequences.
- A paragraph ending with a packaged moral, maxim, or redundant summary instead of stopping at its real point.
- Overcorrection: forced bluntness, clipped one-idea sentences, fake informality, disjointed rhythm, or thesaurus churn.

Preserve any construction that is justified by context. A clear list is often better than a falsely "organic" paragraph; precise technical repetition is often better than synonym rotation.

## Choosing intervention depth

**Local repair:** For one or two isolated issues, preserve the structure and strong language. Replace vague statements with the fact or mechanism, cut echoes, or combine adjacent thoughts.

**Structural reset:** When formulaic organization dominates the passage, privately extract the actual content spine, rank claims or events, and rebuild the order around an actor, observation, constraint, problem, or choice. Restore required abstractions and conventions only after the content structure is sound. Keep the original facts and authorial intention intact.

**Genre-protected:** When the alleged "AI-like" pattern is required by the discipline or destination, leave it alone and explain why only if asked.

## Candidate and feedback protocol

One draft and one focused audit suffice for ordinary writing. For genuinely difficult revisions, compare two candidates that differ in reasoning order or emphasis, not only synonyms. Reject any candidate that changes meaning or violates genre or length before judging style. Keep the stronger version as a rollback copy. Stop when another round merely swaps equivalent phrasings.

A detector's score is at most weak feedback about textual regularities; it is not proof of authorship. Do not target score thresholds, claim a document is undetectable, or distort facts to alter a number. When the user supplies detector comments, translate each useful observation into a defensible craft issue and ignore unsupported authorship claims.

## Final voice check

Read the final passage without viewing a lint result. Does its opening reach the actual subject? Do paragraphs receive space according to intellectual importance? Are genuine qualifications preserved? Does every flourish earn its place? Are source-specific sentences and emotional textures intact? Does the ending stop where the work is finished?
