# Output contracts

Use only the structure that helps the user's particular request; a two-sentence revision rarely needs a report.

## Draft or rewrite

Return the finished text in the requested format. Do not preface it with process narration, mention humanization, or attach unsolicited lists of editorial virtues. Preserve quotations, citations, and formatting. If variants are requested, make them meaningfully different in register, organization, or emphasis.

## Proofread or copyedit

Return the corrected text. For a brief passage with consequential changes, optionally give a short note distinguishing error corrections from judgment calls. Do not flood the reader with a style diary.

## Review without rewriting

Lead with an actionable judgment, then a short set of prioritized findings. Each substantive finding should identify **location**, **evidence**, **problem**, **reader consequence**, **proposed repair**, and **confidence** when uncertainty matters. Distinguish an error from a preference; quote only what is needed.

## Developmental or manuscript audit

State scope and coverage. Summarize the governing structural diagnosis, the strongest elements to preserve, the ranked systemic defects, the dependency-ordered plan, and any section-specific findings. Do not claim that unseen pages were inspected. If useful, provide a reverse outline with unit, function, claim or story turn, evidence/setup, and open thread.

## Style sheet or continuity bible

Use concise records with source locations. A style sheet can hold spelling, punctuation, terminology, hyphenation, capitalization, units, and deliberate exceptions. A continuity bible can hold entities, timeline, known facts, knowledge state, setting rules, and unresolved threads. Distinguish established canon from inference and proposals.

## Argument and evidence audit

Present a claim map, validity assessment, evidentiary strength, unsupported assertions, factual verification status, and corrections. Mark external claims **verified**, **unverified**, or **contradicted** based on actual inspection; never invent source coverage.

## Revision comparison

Identify substantive improvements, regressions, and neutral alternatives; report what should be retained from each version. If a synthesis is requested, return a complete best-of-both revision, then verify protected facts and references.

## Publication readiness

Classify only inspected content: **ready**, **ready after targeted fixes**, **needs another revision pass**, or **needs structural revision**. Say what remains and why; do not promise acceptance or success.
